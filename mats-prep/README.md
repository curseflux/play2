# MATS Winter 2027 — assessment preparation

Preparation material for the two MATS application assessments, plus runnable
practice for the coding one.

**Epistemic status:** I have not seen either test. Everything here is inferred
from the two assessment blurbs, the reasoning-transparency essay they point at,
and what this kind of work actually involves. The process advice follows fairly
directly from what the blurbs say they score, and I'd stand behind it. The
specific scenario content is a guess — useful as a drill, not as a prediction.

## The two things most worth remembering

**Research taste:** build a provenance table before a theory, do the arithmetic the
packet invites, and make an actual recommendation whose justification is robust to
your own uncertainty.

**Coding:** re-decide every frame from the current state, and write your own
randomised test set — on the practice task those two moves are worth 78 of the 85
points.
